Our story

Built by people who used to be short on time, too.

AB Education Consultant started as a small group of graduate writers helping classmates meet deadlines without sacrificing quality. Today we work with college, undergraduate and postgraduate students across Pakistan, on the same principle: know your price, know your writer, get your work on time.

Our mission

Make academic support transparent, not intimidating.

Most students don't need someone to do their thinking for them — they need a second pair of hands when three assignments land in the same week. We charge one honest rate per word, we show writer qualifications up front, and every order is tracked from submission to delivery.

Plagiarism-checked Subject-matched writers Deadline-first scheduling Referencing in APA, MLA, Harvard

Who writes for you

Subject specialists

Postgraduate qualified
